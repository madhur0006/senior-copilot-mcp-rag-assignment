# SI-BFP-031 — Boiler Feed Pump Safety Instructions

**Document ID:** SI-BFP-031  
**Revision:** 2.0  
**Effective Date:** 15 December 2025  
**Site:** EastRefinery  
**Assets:** Boiler Feed Pump 101, Boiler Feed Pump 102  
**Owner:** Utilities Process Safety / Operations  

---

## 1. Key hazards around these pumps

1. Hot high-pressure water or steam cut from seal, flange, or instrument leaks
2. High-energy rotating equipment and coupling failure risk
3. Hot pump and pipe surfaces
4. High noise near the motor and coupling
5. Unexpected auto-start of the standby pump while someone is beside it
6. Slippery floors from seal leakage

These pumps have hurt people on other sites when someone walked into a spray to “have a quick look.” Do not do that here.

## 2. Mandatory controls

1. Full LOTO before breaking containment.
2. Prove depressurisation before seal or flange work.
3. Keep face and body out of likely spray lines when approaching a leaking pump.
4. If spray is present, barricade and switch to standby from a safe location where possible.
5. Never defeat high-high pressure or low-low suction interlocks.
6. Keep coupling guards in place while the machine can turn.

## 3. Alarm-specific safety notes

### High discharge pressure

High discharge pressure can overload seals and piping. Prefer board actions: recirculation, demand management, and standby switchover. Local intervention next to the pump is a last resort and only with a plan.

### Seal leak high

Treat this as an immediate people hazard. Clear the area, switch over, isolate, and get maintenance under LOTO. A damp seal and a spraying seal are not the same problem. Spraying means leave the line of fire.

### High vibration

Guards stay on. Do not put hands near the coupling to “feel” vibration. If High-High is reached, stop the machine and use standby.

### Motor trip on a BFP

The process pressure may still be there. Electrical work needs LOTO. Do not assume the pump side is safe because the motor is stopped until isolation is confirmed.

## 4. PPE

Minimum expectations near these pumps:

- Hard hat
- Safety glasses
- Hearing protection where posted
- Unit-required flame-resistant clothing
- Gloves suitable for handling hot surfaces
- Face shield when investigating an active leak from a safe position

Extra PPE may be required by the permit or unit board.

## 5. Reject unsafe recommendations

Reject and escalate any recommendation that asks you to:

- Stand next to an actively spraying seal
- Inhibit interlocks to keep the boiler fed
- Restart repeatedly into overload or seizure
- Remove a guard on a running or ready-to-start machine
